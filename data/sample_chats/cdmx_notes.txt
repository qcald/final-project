Mexico City notes from my trip last year:
- Contramar for lunch, the tuna tostadas are the best thing I ate all trip. Book ahead.
- Museo Nacional de Antropología, give it a full morning
- Café Avellaneda in Coyoacán, tiny but the best coffee
- Lucha libre at Arena México on a Tuesday night, super fun
- El Huequito tacos al pastor, cheap and great
- Xochimilco was kind of a letdown honestly, crowded and loud
